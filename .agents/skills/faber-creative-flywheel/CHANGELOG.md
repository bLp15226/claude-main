**Added to `references/brief-templates.md` - production notes:**
- **Finish bar for test creative (FE-1).** A legitimately-produced asset may ship at low production value
  because the test decides, not the polish. **The caveat is load-bearing and is not to be trimmed:** cheap
  production does NOT raise the bloat-guard ceiling on live-ad count - the constraint was never difficulty
  of production, it is the budget's ability to give each asset meaningful spend. Explicitly distinguished
  from the **rejected** Karolis bar (`faber-video-karolis-ai-ugc.md:46-48`), which was a bar on *deception*
  (indistinguishability from a real customer); this is a bar on *finish*, for assets legitimate to begin with.

**Added to `references/brief-templates.md` - proof rule:**
- **Which ground does the work (FE-3).** The refusal rests on two independent grounds: the
  **fabricated-presenter** ground (a synthetic figure presented as a person, refused *as such*, regardless of
  what it says) and the **fabricated-testimonial** ground (a first-person experience claim = an endorsement,
  the FTC case). The edge case has now actually occurred: Eclipse's synthetic presenter delivers a
  *spokesperson read* with **no experience claim**, so ground 2 does not catch it - ground 1 does. A
  **likeness** problem is a third, separate matter surviving both. Third firing of this rule; first without
  a testimony claim.

**Filed as a reference - named ethics case study (FE-2):**
- **"Identifies the ethical line out loud, then steps over it."** Three confirmed rejections from one source
  - watermark-stripped competitor creative run as paid ads (the speaker names the copyright problem and

  unread AI-generated infant-sleep advice at $24.99. The connecting pattern - accurate awareness of each
  boundary at the moment of crossing - is recognition material for future intake. Includes the deliberate
  **negative finding**: the same source used no countdown, no fake stock, no invented review, no fake price
  anchor. A source can be disqualifying on sourcing and clean on persuasion.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-19
Initial install. Written directly following house conventions (not routed through Skill Forge — the spec
was fully determined by the source review; novelty was checked manually against the installed library).

**Wedge:** PHC had `legatus-meta-ads` reading ad performance and `scriptor` writing copy, with nothing
connecting them. Ad results never fed back into creative production. Faber owns that loop and only that
loop — it consumes Legatus's classification and hands words to Scriptor, so it overlaps neither.

**Source:** Meta Ads course transcript (Sam Piliero / The Moonlighters, ~52 min), reviewed 2026-07-19.
Adopted the creative-flywheel structure and the winner-iterate / potential-vary split. Rejected or adapted
the rest — see below.

- Consumes Legatus's winner/potential lists as **separate** inputs; merging them is an integrity violation,
  since the two classes earn deliberately opposite creative treatment (narrow iteration vs. wide variation).
- **Account-bloat guard** — the source's own closing warning was that AI creative loops bloat ad accounts
  into a "death loop" of ads that never earn enough spend to be judged, but it shipped no mechanism to
  prevent it. Faber derives one: `(daily budget × 14) ÷ $100 classification floor − live ads = new
  variations allowed`. If the result is ≤0, the correct output is no briefs at all. Documented limitation:
  the formula assumes even spend distribution and therefore runs **optimistic under CBO**, which
  concentrates budget on winners by design — the result is a ceiling, not a target.
- **Fabricated-proof prohibition** — the source cheerfully suggests AI-generating UGC and testimonial-style
  ads. Given PHC's fake-review-wipe history and the FTC Consumer Reviews Rule's civil penalties for
  fabricated endorsements, this is a hard refusal here, including for test creative. Every brief template
  carries a mandatory `Proof used:` line. This is the largest deliberate divergence from the source.
- Briefs must name a specific Augur avatar (never "our customer") and state the **one variable** being
  tested; a variation that changes five things teaches nothing when it wins.
- Creative direction only — finished copy routes to `scriptor`, preserving the same boundary Legatus holds.
- Claude does not generate ad images; generation routes to a connected MCP or a human creator, and the
  route is always stated. The source's specific tool picks (Super Scale, Higgs Field, Nano Banana Pro) were
  **not** adopted — they're one agency's stack and would need independent evaluation.
- 14-day run window, then hand back to Legatus for re-classification. A cycle that produces no learning is
  reported as run wrong, not as a neutral result.
- `references/brief-templates.md` holds the winner/potential/video-script templates, the reasoning behind
  the split, the proof rule, and the cycle log for cross-session diffing.

**Not yet verified live** — no flywheel cycle has been run against the real account. Thresholds inherited
from Legatus are still generic placeholders.

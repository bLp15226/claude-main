> costs real creative work — every asset would be framed to a constraint that does not exist.

**The claim.** Per this source, Meta recently changed asset handling: separate creatives can no
longer be uploaded per placement size. Instead upload **one 16×9 asset, vertical, with a 4×5 safe
zone**, and Meta renders it across formats. He characterises it as a significant change that is
not being widely discussed. *STATED · PLATFORM MECHANICS · DEMONSTRATED PER NARRATION.*

**Why it matters to Faber if true.** This is a **briefing-time constraint, not a post-production
one**. The composition must survive cropping to 4×5 while being delivered as 16×9 — meaning the
subject, product and any text have to sit inside the narrower safe area from the moment the shot is
framed. A brief that ignores it produces assets that crop badly in feed, and the failure is
discovered after the asset exists.

**Verification standard before this becomes doctrine.** Confirm against Meta's own advertising
documentation or the live ads-manager upload flow through the connector. Two independent grounds
for caution: it is one practitioner's account of a recent change, and the video was two days old at
extraction — recency cuts both ways, since a genuinely new change is also the most likely thing to
be reported imprecisely.

**Related, same status.** Meta can show an individual uploaded ad alongside catalog products, so

uploaded ads; carousel and collection display can be toggled off if that presentation is unwanted.

## 2 — The Advantage+ enhancement rule

**The heuristic, which is the durable part:**

> **If it alters the media, off. If it only enhances around the media, on.**

His stated application at time of recording:

| Off — alters the media | On — enhances around it |
|---|---|
| Cropping, colour change | Overlays, dynamic overlays |
| Conversion to video, animation | Dynamic description, text improvements |
| Visual touch-ups, product touch-ups | Enhanced CTA, show summaries |
| Generated backgrounds, music | Relevant comments, spotlights |
| Brightness adjustment | Reveal-details-over-time |

*STATED · PLATFORM MECHANICS · ASSERTED.* He notes explicitly that these options change constantly
and that the rule matters more than the list.

> **PHC synthesis — keep the rule, treat the table as perishable.** A list of 2026 checkbox names
> will rot within months; the heuristic survives the churn, which is the whole reason it is worth
> recording rather than the settings themselves.
>
> **There is also an independent PHC rationale the video does not give, and it is the stronger one
> for Faber specifically:** letting Meta alter the media silently **invalidates creative testing**.
> If the asset being served is not the asset that was briefed, then a winner/potential
> classification is measuring something Faber did not produce and cannot deliberately reproduce.
> The flywheel depends on the tested artefact and the briefed artefact being the same object.

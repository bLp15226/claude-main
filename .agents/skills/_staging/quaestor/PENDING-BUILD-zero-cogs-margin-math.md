## 1. The zero-COGS break-even rule

**STATED · ASSERTED** [15:15, restated 17:29]. With no cost of goods and no shipping, the
only per-order variable cost is ad spend plus payment fees, so the break-even test collapses
to one comparison:

> **cost per purchase < selling price**

The speaker watches this single number across the whole test and treats staying under the
$24.99 price as the definition of profitable.

**PHC caution, to travel with this rule if it is ever adopted (INFERENCE):** this is not a
general margin rule — it is the special case of a COGS-free product. PHC's catalogue is
physical and landed-cost-bearing. Applied to a PHC product the same comparison would
**overstate profitability by the entire COGS line**, and PHC already holds a stricter standard
in the 3x landed-cost rule (`scriptor/references/scriptor-doctrine-collier.md:194`,
`beowulf-product-scouting/SKILL.md`). **The rule and its condition must never be separated.**

## 2. Front-end / back-end margin structure

**STATED · ASSERTED** [08:47]. A COGS-free entry offer acquires the customer; margin is
protected by a post-purchase upsell of physical goods carrying real COGS. Observed attach:
**1 of 8 orders** — a single $19.99 white-noise machine.

The *offer-language* half of this routed to Scriptor
(`scriptor/references/scriptor-video-eclipse-ai-digital-product-72h.md` §2), where the
placement rule already exists and is stricter. **What is held here is the arithmetic**: how a
front-end priced at or below CAC is underwritten by back-end attach rate × back-end margin,
and what attach rate makes that structure solvent. The video never computes this — it reports
one attach and moves on.

## 3. The reported figures — and why they do not reconcile

| Day | Spend | Orders | Revenue | Net stated | Margin stated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | $50 | 3 | $94.96 | $30 | 31% |
| 3 | $100 | 5 | $184.93 | $45 | — |
| **Total** | **$150** | **8** | **$279.89** | **$75** | **26%** |

**Recomputed by PHC, not taken on narration:**

- **Day 2 reconciles.** $94.96 − $50 spend − ~$15 of fees and upsell COGS ≈ $30. Consistent.
- **The 72-hour total does not.** $279.89 − $150 = $129.89 before fees. A stated $75 net
  implies roughly **$55 of unexplained cost**. Payment processing on ~$280 of card volume runs
  nearer $10–12; the single $19.99 upsell carries an AliExpress COGS that is never disclosed.
  Neither closes a $55 gap. **The discrepancy is not resolvable from the video.**
- **The headline margin is internally consistent** — $75 on $279.89 is 26.8%, so "26% net
  margins" checks out *against the stated net*. It is the path from $150 of spend to $75 of
  net that is unshown.

**All PERFORMANCE claims — provisional, and none is a threshold.** n=8 over three days. Day 3
is one day at a doubled budget; the difference between five orders and three is noise. No
number in this file may become a PHC threshold, benchmark, or planning assumption.

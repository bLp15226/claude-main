## The held content

**Private supplier in place of a public marketplace.** He uses HyperSKU as a private supplier
rather than sourcing from a public marketplace listing. The workflow as described:

1. Send the supplier a **screenshot of the product being sold**.
2. The supplier sources it and links it to the Shopify store.
3. Orders placed on Shopify appear on the supplier's dashboard.
4. He pays per order; the supplier ships direct to the customer. He never handles inventory.

**Landed cost breakdown, as shown:** $3.36 product + $9.18 shipping = **$12.54 total**, at
**5–9 day** delivery. *STATED · PLATFORM MECHANICS · DEMONSTRATED PER NARRATION (the supplier
dashboard).*

**Claimed advantages:** cheaper and better quality than public marketplace sourcing.
*CLAIMED · zero evidence offered.* He asserts both; the video shows neither a price comparison
nor any quality assessment. Treat as unverified vendor-adjacent claim.

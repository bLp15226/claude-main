**4.2 ⚠ Corroboration or echo? State this honestly.**

creator and **predates** that ingestion (2026-06-30 vs. 2026-07-19). On the surface that is clean

**But both operators work in the same dropshipping-education ecosystem, and these may simply be
community-standard figures both inherited from a common upstream source.** If so, "two sources
agree" carries almost no additional evidential weight — it is one belief counted twice.

> **PHC position, per the Three Laws (verified over plausible):** treat this as **weak
> corroboration at best**, not as validation of the thresholds. The numbers remain what they
> already were — placeholders sourced from general media-buying practice, awaiting replacement by
> PHC's own account data. **This changes nothing about their status and must not be used to argue
> they are now confirmed.** INFERENCE.

**4.3 The genuinely new item — an early gate at ROAS > 0.8.**

A 48-hour ROAS gate of 0.8 is **deliberately below break-even**. It is not a profitability
threshold; it is an early **survival** signal — a check that the campaign is not dead on arrival,
run long before profitability can be judged.

> **PHC has no equivalent concept.** Legatus's ROAS work is target-ROAS for winner classification

> continuing?"
>
> The specific number 0.8 is unevidenced and should not be adopted. **The concept — a sub-break-even
> early gate distinct from the profitability threshold — is the contribution.** INFERENCE.

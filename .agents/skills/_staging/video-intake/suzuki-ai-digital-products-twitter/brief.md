ETHICS FILTER REJECTED:
- Unsolicited automated DMs at ~10,000/day — every liker auto-DM'd a payment link. A like is
  not a request to be solicited. THIS IS THE DISTRIBUTION MECHANISM, so it can't be stripped
  and the method kept.
- Disabling comments on sales posts ("so people actually aren't able to comment") —
  suppression of adverse information on a post whose function is to sell. PHC deleted 1,775
  fake reviews to get out from under this exact class of problem.
- Contempt as the segmentation criterion — non-targets are "very unintelligent," other
  platforms' users are "dumb, broke people." Principle kept, criterion rejected: it will leak
  into the copy, and PHC's ethos is the opposite.
- The retweet circle — 70+ accounts reciprocally boosting on demand, sold as a coaching perk.
  Coordinated inauthentic amplification, and it inflates the very outcomes he credits to his
  teaching.
- 150–200 page ebooks generated in "2 minutes," bundled five at a time, sold at $350–400. Same
  ground as the Eclipse rejection, more severe — nothing suggests a human reads them.
- "More than an 85% chance you'll make at least 10K per month" attached to his paid coaching.
  A specific income claim selling a business-opportunity program, zero substantiation in 42
  minutes.
- FLAGGED not rejected: he argues X is the "most secure" platform because moderation is lax —
  "you can post gore, you can post porn, and no one gets banned" — and says he left LinkedIn
  because it banned accounts. A model whose durability rests on non-enforcement is carrying an
  unpriced risk, not an absence of one.

## Held items

**D1 — Digital delivery is app-layer, not theme-layer.** [08:11] A digital-download app (named
on screen as **BIG Digital Download**) attaches the file to the product and emails the buyer a
download link on purchase. The buyer clicks through from the email to the file. ·
DEMONSTRATED per narration · described as quick to set up.

**D2 — Post-purchase upsell is a separate app again.** [08:35] A post-purchase upsell app
(named as **AfterSell**) presents physical add-ons *after* the purchase completes. The
add-ons were sourced on AliExpress and imported through AutoDS. · DEMONSTRATED per narration.

**D3 — Listing a digital product as a physical one.** [04:45] He adds the PDF to Shopify as an
ordinary product with an uploaded image, then lets the download app handle delivery. He flags
this as a wrinkle — *"we still need to add that PDF onto our shop as a physical product"* —
without explaining the consequences (shipping profile, tax treatment, fulfilment status).
**Those consequences are unexamined in the video and are not recorded here as fact.**

## Vendor naming — read this before using any name above

The two app names and AutoDS are recorded **as they were narrated**, for traceability only.
This is not an endorsement, an evaluation, or a recommendation of any of them, and none was
verified — no pricing, no capability, no current availability was checked, and no frames were
sampled. **AutoDS has sponsored this speaker's channel for three years (disclosed on camera
at 09:13)**, so its appearance in the video is a paid placement rather than a comparison
result.

## Why no owner, and why that was the right call

Centurion was the plausible candidate — checkout-path integrity is its lane — but the actual
content is **app selection and post-purchase offer construction** for a product category PHC
does not sell. Filing it into Centurion would have put digital-goods fulfilment advice inside
the skill that audits whether PHC's physical checkout works, which is a lane the ruling
declined to blur for one video's material.

## Revisit condition

every item above as **unverified and dated 2026-07-20** — app capabilities and pricing move
fast, and D3's tax and shipping consequences would need answering properly before anything
ships.

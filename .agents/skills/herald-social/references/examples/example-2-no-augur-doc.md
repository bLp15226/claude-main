# Golden example 2 — No existing Augur research yet

## Input
"Hey Herald, what should we post this week for the Vote pillar?" Context: no foundational doc exists yet
for the relevant product.

## A great output must assert
- Flags plainly that no Augur research exists yet for this product — doesn't silently proceed as if it has
  real customer data.
- Suggests invoking Augur for deeper grounding, without blocking the request on it.
- Still drafts best-effort content from the Vote pillar's own tone/general brand ethos rather than refusing
  or stalling entirely.
- Doesn't fabricate a customer insight or persona to fill the gap Augur would normally fill.

## Why this is the bar
This tests the graceful-degradation path — the realistic early-stage case for most of PHC's catalog, per
Augur's own documented context (most products don't have research yet). A mediocre output would either
silently invent audience assumptions to sound more grounded than it is, or refuse to draft anything at all.

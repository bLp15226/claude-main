# Golden example 4 — Post-mortem on launch + proactive confidence-upgrade

## Input
"Hey Augur, the Aurelia throw blanket launched a month ago — turns out the audience really responded to the
'cozy morning' angle way more than the gift-giving angle we predicted. Also, we've been running ads on the
Coastal Candle for two weeks now and it's converting well."

Context: Aurelia's foundational doc (built pre-launch) predicted gift-giving as the primary motivation,
hypothesis-stage. Coastal Candle has a separate, older hypothesis-stage doc that Ben didn't ask about
directly.

## A great output must assert
- **Produces a post-mortem for Aurelia**: predicted (gift-giving angle, from the doc), actual (cozy-morning
  angle won), names specifically where the research was right/wrong, and a reusable confidence-calibration
  takeaway (not a vague "personas are hard").
- **Proactively flags Coastal Candle for a refresh** even though Ben didn't ask about it directly — noticed
  in passing that real ad-performance signal now exists for a product with a hypothesis-stage doc, and says
  so unprompted rather than staying silent.
- Keeps the two responses clearly separated — doesn't blend the post-mortem and the proactive flag into one
  muddled answer.
- Doesn't fabricate specifics for either product beyond what Ben actually stated or what real data would
  show.

## Why this is the bar
This is where the two accepted ideas (A3 post-mortem, C1 proactive upgrade) have to work together: the
post-mortem closes the loop on a hypothesis that's now been tested, while the proactive flag demonstrates
Augur isn't just reactive — it's watching for refresh opportunities across the conversation, not just
answering the literal question asked.

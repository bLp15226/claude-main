## v2.1.0 — 2026-07-28
Minor — one new procedure with a mandatory constraint, plus three integrity rules. No breaking changes.
Routed through the `/watch` **Aurelius routing gate**. Source: Blackie, "I Tried Dropshipping From Scratch
For 7 Days" (2026-07-27) — see `references/augur-video-blackie-7day-dropship.md`.

**Added — "Demand validation: a separate Ad Library procedure" (A-1, A-2, A-3):**
- **The three-step check** — find a *sponsored* post carrying organic-scale engagement (the
  sponsored-vs-organic distinction is the point: a paid unit only accrues that engagement if it keeps
  earning funding), confirm months of live ads in the Ad Library, then check creative volume and variety as
  evidence of active testing and scaling. The inference underneath: nobody funds months of varied creative
  on an unprofitable product.
- **Kept deliberately separate from the sophistication read.** The hunt already uses the Ad Library, and
  `foundational-docs-taxonomy.md` already notes long-running ads are ads that work — but that answers *what
  are competitors claiming*, a messaging question feeding the sophistication stage. **Same tool, different
  question.** Ruled not to merge them; a new integrity rule requires saying which one is running.
- **Demand filter, not fit filter (A-2) — mandatory, applied in the same edit as A-1.** The check proves a
  product sells *for someone*, not that it sells for PHC at PHC's price to PHC's avatar with PHC's creative.
  Without this constraint the procedure quietly becomes a product-approval rubric, which is the failure it
  exists to block. Also records the structural bias: it selects for products already being worked by capable
  operators — the point when validating demand, a liability when seeking differentiation.
- **Lane boundary added.** Augur's existing "Do NOT use" list excludes raw demand validation on a
  not-yet-chosen product (`beowulf-product-scouting`'s lane, still staged). **That exclusion stands** — this
  procedure runs on a candidate already inside Augur's research scope, never as a scouting sweep.
- **Spy-tool note (A-3)** logged explicitly **hypothesis-stage**: the evidence is one failed product test,
  which is not evidence. The feed-then-Library *sequencing* argument stands on its own reasoning; the
  "spy tools are saturated" claim does not. Watch, do not follow.

**Declined, with reasoning recorded:** the source's stated "solves a problem" screening heuristic and its
demographic-resistance targeting rationale. **The source's own case study disproves the first** — the filter
he claims to use produced the product that failed, while his winner (an emotional gift item) solved no
problem at all. The second conflicts with PHC's brand ethos and also did not produce the winner. A screening
heuristic contradicted by its own evidence is not adopted.

## v2.0.0 — 2026-07-19
**Major — merges the standalone `augur` skill into this one.** Breaking: the persistent doc goes from four
sections to six, so previously-built docs are incomplete against the new shape.

**Why:** two skills both answered "who's the avatar for X" — `augur` (live hunt → research packet for
Scriptor) and `augur-research` (persistent taxonomy → read by Scriptor, Herald, Legatus). Any research
request was a coin flip between them, which made routing unreliable and would have mis-fired the first time
a research video came through the `video-to-hat` pipeline. Ben's call: one Augur.

They turned out to be two halves of one job rather than duplicates, and each fixed the other's weakness:
- `augur-research` step 2 said "gather available signal" with **no method**, so it defaulted to category
  reasoning — hypothesis-stage by omission rather than by finding.
- `augur` had **no persistence**, no confidence labels, and no staleness checks, so it re-hunted the same
  ground every time.

Merged so research compounds instead of repeating.

- **Absorbed the hunt** as the documented method behind Workflow step 2: customer language (Reddit, 3-star
  reviews, forums, YouTube comments), competitor claims (Meta Ad Library, long-running ads), congregations
  (named handles, real search strings). New integrity rule: **hunt before reasoning** — category knowledge
  is the documented fallback, not the default path.
- **Taxonomy 4 → 6 sections.** Added **Sophistication Stage** (1-5 + mechanism/claim/identification-led
  recommendation) and **Congregation Map** (named handles and search strings). These were the two things
  the 4-part shape couldn't express, and dropping them to preserve a "four-part" label would have chosen
  tidiness over function — Scriptor's headline strategy depends on the stage call, and PHC's traffic
  targeting depends on the congregation map.
- **Desire scoring folded into Motivations & Objections** rather than becoming a seventh section, keeping
  its discipline (intensity / staying power / scope, then name the ONE dominant desire to lead with).
- **Language Patterns now carries the phrasebook** — two clusters (away-from-pain / toward-pleasure) of
  literal customer sentences with sources cited, 10-20 minimum or flagged thin.
- **New integrity rules:** never hedge the sophistication call (a labeled hypothesis-stage guess is usable
  downstream, "it depends" is not); congregations must be named, not platforms; verbatim phrases need real
  cited sources.
- Output format gained a `Hunt:` line reporting what was actually pulled — this exists to make *skipping*
  the hunt visible, since every section carries a confidence label either way and the difference would
  otherwise be invisible.
- `handoffs_to` +`scriptor` (the relationship was real but undeclared — Scriptor was `augur`'s entire
  reason for existing).
- **Staleness sweep:** example 3 updated for the six-part shape and the new hunt-first requirement.
  Examples 1, 2, and 4 re-read and still valid — they turn on signal-thinness, staleness refresh, and
  post-mortem behavior, none of which changed. `legatus-meta-ads`, `faber-creative-flywheel`, and
  `herald-social` reference Augur by name only, not by section count — no edits needed.
- **The old `augur` skill is deactivated, not deleted:** `augur/SKILL.md` → `augur/SKILL.md.superseded`.
  It stops loading (so the trigger collision is gone) but the content is preserved and one `mv` restores
  it. Not deleted outright because it wasn't mine to destroy.

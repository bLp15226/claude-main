## 1. THE PRIME LAWS (the canon distilled to 7 rules)

These override everything downstream. When in doubt, come back here.

1. **The market beats the copy.** Halbert's starving crowd: the single greatest advantage is not a better burger, a better location, or better copy — it's a hungry audience. Before writing a word, confirm the desire already exists. Copy channels desire; it never creates it. (This is the same law as Schwartz: mass desire pre-exists.)
2. **Advertising is salesmanship in print** (Hopkins). Every line must earn its keep the way a door-to-door salesman must. No cleverness for its own sake, no brand-poetry that a live salesman would never say to a prospect's face. Test: would you say this sentence out loud to one person across a table?
3. **Enter the conversation already going on in the prospect's mind** (Collier). Never open with the product. Open with what they were already thinking about at the moment your message arrived — their pain, their day, their private worry. The product enters as the answer to a thought they were already having.
4. **Specificity is believability** (Hopkins/Caples). "Deposits $59,436.26" beats "makes great money." Round numbers read as claims; exact numbers read as facts. Every claim in PHC copy gets a number, a timeframe, or a concrete detail — or gets cut.
5. **Research is 80% of the writing** (Halbert). Halbert's process was overwhelmingly fact-finding: read everything about the market, the product, the competition, then let the ad assemble itself from the facts. Scriptor never writes from a blank page — it writes from a fact/avatar file.
6. **One reader, one letter** (Halbert/Collier). The best-performing format in history is the personal letter: written to one person, in spoken English, at Reader's Digest reading level. Even a Facebook ad to 100K people is written to one person in their 50s with a bad back, sitting at their kitchen table.
7. **Motion beats meditation** (Halbert). Fast, rough first drafts; edit after. Scriptor drafts hot and edits cold — never polishes sentence one while sentence two is unwritten.

## 2. BOOK-BY-BOOK EXTRACTION

### 2.1 The Boron Letters (Halbert)
> **PROVENANCE: PRIMARY = `scriptor-doctrine-halbert-boron.md`. Drift-audited and corrected 2026-07-23.** Where the two disagree, the primary wins.
- **A-pile / B-pile:** all mail gets sorted into personal (opened always) and commercial (tossed). Every piece of PHC communication must feel A-pile: personal, from a human, low-gloss. Applies to email subject lines and FB ad openers — the digital A-pile is "this looks like it's for me, from a person," the B-pile is "this looks like marketing."
- **AIDA** as the base skeleton: Attention (headline/hook) → Interest (facts, story) → Desire (benefits, proof, future-pacing) → Action (explicit, simple, immediate CTA). Scriptor checks every draft against AIDA in order.
- **Sell what they want to buy.** Don't sell what you wish they wanted. Mine the market for its existing appetite (ties to FB Ad Library research doctrine — validated signal = existing appetite).
- **Hand-copy discipline (strict original, restored 2026-07-23):** Halbert's actual prescription is literal — copy proven ads out in your own handwriting, about one per week, sustained 4–5 months, paired with reading copy aloud until it flows without a stumble; the unpleasantness of the discipline is the moat, because competitors won't suffer it. (An earlier version of this bullet had softened it to "re-read specimens" — corrected per the Boron deep read.) Scriptor's AI translation: before drafting in a new format, load 2–3 road-tested specimens of that format and match cadence, never content.
- **Grease-slide copy:** every sentence's only job is to get the next sentence read. Short sentences. Short paragraphs. Bucket-brigade connectors ("Here's why...", "But that's not all...", "Now, listen:", "And get this —").
- **Eliminate "work words":** any phrase that makes the reader do mental labor (jargon, abstractions, long clauses) gets rewritten in kitchen-table English.

### 2.3 Tested Advertising Methods (Caples)
> **PROVENANCE: SECONDHAND, NO PRIMARY AVAILABLE — TREAT AS UNVERIFIED.** No Caples primary exists in references/; these claims cannot be audited against source and must not be presented as sourced doctrine.
- **Headline appeals in order of power: self-interest > news > curiosity.** Curiosity alone is the weakest and most dangerous; the best headlines combine self-interest with news or curiosity ("New" + benefit; "How to" + benefit; question aimed at their exact situation).
- **Proven headline skeletons** Scriptor may reach for: "How to [benefit] without [pain]" / "Which of these [problems] do you want to fix?" / "To [avatar identity] who wants [desire]" / "Advice to [avatar]" / "[Number] ways to [benefit]" / "Warning:" / "At last," / "Announcing".
- **The first paragraph carries the headline forward** — never restarts, never throat-clears. If the headline is a promise, paragraph one begins delivering it.
- **Long copy outsells short when interest exists.** Length is never the issue; boredom is. For considered purchases (which most $60–$150 PHC products are), completeness beats brevity.
- **Don't be clever. Be clear.** Puns, wordplay, and "creative" ads win awards and lose money.

### 2.4 The Robert Collier Letter Book
> **PROVENANCE: PRIMARY = `scriptor-doctrine-collier.md`. Drift-audited and corrected 2026-07-23.** Where the two disagree, the primary wins.
- **The opening joins the reader's world, then earns the next paragraph.** Collier's letters open inside the reader's own moment — season, news, a shared frustration — and his stated jobs for the opening are two: join the train of thought already running AND excite curiosity to read on. Scriptor's email application: first line about *them*, product no earlier than line three *(the line-three timing is PHC synthesis — Collier states no line-number rule)*.
- **Six prime motives, braided — not one.** Love/family, gain, duty, pride, self-indulgence, self-preservation. Collier's operating rule is to STACK them: pride alone rarely opens a wallet — reinforce the lead motive with supporting ones. And he separates the desire motive from the action motive: what makes her *want* it and what makes her *act now* are different levers; the close sells the action motive. *(Corrected 2026-07-23 — this bullet previously said "one dominant motive per piece," which is Schwartz's rule wearing Collier's name.)*
- **Reconciliation — do not "resolve" this again:** Schwartz and Collier operate at different levels. Schwartz governs the ONE mass desire a piece channels strategically (that rule stands, attributed to Schwartz). Collier governs the supporting motives braided tactically inside the piece. Both are correct; they are not in conflict.
- **The mind thinks in pictures.** Don't describe the product; screen the movie of the reader using it and living the after-state. Collier's support: switching from intellect-appeal to emotional/story description tripled and quadrupled returns on the same offer. The rule that future-pacing paragraphs are *mandatory* in sales pages and long emails is **PHC prescription built on that finding, not Collier's own rule**.

### 2.5 How to Write a Good Advertisement (Vic Schwab)
> **PROVENANCE: SECONDHAND, NO PRIMARY AVAILABLE — TREAT AS UNVERIFIED.** No Schwab primary exists in references/; these claims cannot be audited against source and must not be presented as sourced doctrine.

### 2.7 Scott Haines sales letter (specimen dissection — the letter itself, not the course)
> **PROVENANCE: SPECIMEN — the dissected letter is not archived in references/. SECONDHAND, TREAT AS UNVERIFIED** until the letter file lands; block-level claims cannot currently be re-checked.
What the letter *demonstrates*, block by block:
- **Headline stack:** superscript (context/qualifier) → main headline (big promise, power verbs: "reveals," "unleash") → immediate authority quote (Halbert endorsement) before the salutation. Three layers deep before "Dear Friend."
- **Story lead:** the answering-machine story is a masterclass — specific, humanizing, slightly self-deprecating, name-drops naturally, and takes 10+ paragraphs before selling anything. Interest is built entirely on narrative before the pitch begins.
- **"Slightly bad" credibility:** admits shyness, admits the brother thought he was a joke, admits he was a nobody. Small flaws make big claims believable.
- **Bullet mechanics (the letter's core skill):** mostly *blind* bullets (curiosity + benefit, mechanism concealed), each with a specific hook (a number, a named source, a forbidden angle), many with parenthetical intensifiers. Bullets carry the middle 60% of the letter. Rhythm: short bullet, long bullet, short, short, long.
- **Proof stacking:** testimonials with full names + city/state, exact dollar figures ($59,436.26, 4% response at $1,032/unit, 15:1 ROI), before-states ("living in my Nissan Pathfinder"), and authority endorsements from named legends. Every proof element is *specific and attributable*.
- **Price presentation:** value build (what it cost him to learn: $50K+, decades) → price reveal as contrast → bonus stack (course + method guide + swipe collection) → per-item value framing.
- **P.S. does real work** (logistics + reassurance), not decoration.
- ⚠️ **PHC ethics flag:** the letter's "price going up soon, don't say I didn't warn you" close is exactly the manufactured-urgency pattern PHC bans — unless the price change is real and dated. Scriptor may model everything above EXCEPT unverifiable urgency and may never invent testimonials (Founder's First Order).

## 3. THE SCRIPTOR WRITING SEQUENCE (updated master process)

1. **Fact file first.** Product facts, avatar file (Section 6), awareness stage, sophistication stage, dominant emotion, one prime motive. No file → run deep-dive or call Augur. Never draft cold.
2. **Bullets and headline candidates before body.** Write 10–25 bullets from the fact file; write 10+ headline candidates (Caples skeletons + Schwab reward-promise test). The strongest bullet often IS the headline.
3. **Draft hot** (Halbert): full draft, fast, spoken English, no editing mid-flow.
4. **Edit cold, next session if possible:** cut work words, shorten sentences, insert bucket-brigade connectors, verify AIDA and Schwab's five steps, read aloud — anything you stumble on gets rewritten. **Eye relief check** (Halbert's coinage, Boron 17–18): paragraphs 1–3 lines, white space, sentence-length variety, parenthetical rest stops, clean mobile rendering — the page must *look* easy before the words get their chance.
5. **Audit pass:** specificity check (every claim numbered/concrete), proof check (real only), ethics check (no fake urgency/scarcity, no unverified claims), congruence check (headline promise fulfilled in paragraph one and in the offer).

## 4. PER-FORMAT PLAYBOOKS

### 4.1 Email
- **Subject line = headline** but tuned for the A-pile: lowercase-adjacent, personal, curiosity + self-interest, 2–7 words beats clever. Write 10, pick 1.
- **First line is about the reader** (Collier), visible in preview text — treat subject + preview as one unit.
- **One idea, one emotion, one CTA per email.** Chains, not encyclopedias.
- **Letter voice:** first person, contractions, short paragraphs (1–3 lines), grease-slide connectors.
- **CTA appears mid-email and at end;** same link, same ask.
- **P.S. always** — restate offer, deadline (real ones only), or add the one detail that handles the top objection.
- Length: 150–400 words for nurture; longer allowed when the story earns it.

### 4.2 Facebook ad
- **The first line is the entire ad.** It must stop the scroll by naming the avatar's situation or pattern-interrupting inside their conversation (Collier applied to the feed): "If your lower back has a 'don't bend like that' rule..." beats any product statement.
- **Match awareness stage:** cold traffic = problem/story lead, no product name up top; warm = benefit lead; hot = offer lead. (Schwartz five stages govern; this file adds the classic execution.)
- **Ad = the letter's first act only.** Its job is the click, not the sale. Open loop → landing page closes it. Headline promise on the ad must be *fulfilled immediately* on the page (congruence, or CPC and trust both die).
- **Short vs long:** short-copy ads for hot/warm; long "mini-letter" ads (Halbert story style) are legitimate and often win for cold audiences — test both (Hopkins).
- **Ethics guardrail:** no fake countdowns, no "only 3 left," no fabricated social proof. Real reasons why only.

### 4.3 Sales page / product page
- Runs on the existing **12-block anatomy**; this doctrine upgrades the blocks:
  - Headline block = Haines-style stack (superscript qualifier / main promise / subscript proof-tease).
  - Lead block = Collier opening or Halbert story lead or Karbo confession, chosen by awareness stage.
  - Body = reason-why + story hybrid, bullets carrying the middle, future-pacing picture paragraphs.
  - Proof block = specific, attributable, real only. Until PHC has real reviews: proof = the §8.2 evidence hierarchy (mechanism research, verifiable specs, active guarantee, honest concessions). *(The transparency-story-as-proof option was retired 2026-07-23 per the voice ruling — the deleted-reviews story does not appear in customer-facing copy.)*
  - Offer block = value build → price contrast → honest reason-why for any discount.
  - Guarantee block = active, dramatized, specific (see 4.6).
  - Close = Schwab step 5, upgraded per Boron (2026-07-23): ask plainly, then lead by the hand — step-by-step what to click, what they'll see next, what they'll pay, what arrives when, restated once for skimmers. Closes are routinely under-weighted; err heavier than feels natural. No percentage quota (Halbert's 25%+ was his sometimes-practice, not a rule).
  - P.S.-equivalent = final block restating promise + guarantee for skimmers.

### 4.4 Headlines (all formats)
Checklist every headline must pass:
1. Promises a reward for reading (Schwab).
2. Contains self-interest, ideally + news or curiosity (Caples).
3. Specific over general.
4. Could be said out loud to one person without embarrassment (Hopkins).
5. The body copy can and does cash the check it writes.

### 4.5 Bullets (all formats)
- Formula: **benefit + curiosity + specificity**, mechanism blind.
- Vary rhythm; use parenthetical intensifiers sparingly (Haines uses them constantly — at PHC volume, 1 in 3 bullets max).
- The best bullet is a headline candidate.

### 4.6 Offers & guarantees
- Guarantee is a *selling tool*, not fine print: name it, dramatize it, make it active ("use it for 60 nights; if your mornings aren't easier, we refund and you keep the bonus").
- Remove "chicken-shit" qualifiers (Haines): no "must be in original packaging"-style hedges in the sales copy.
- Every deadline/discount states an honest reason why — launch date, inventory batch, founding-member cap that is actually enforced (Founding 50 qualifies: it's real and capped). *(Attribution corrected 2026-07-23: grounded on Collier/Karbo/Sugarman + the Powers lineage, not Hopkins — see §2.2.)*
- **Guarantee framing: forward trust, not refund mechanics (added 2026-07-23, Hopkins Life §2.1–2.2 + Maslen concurring).** Phrase the guarantee from confidence — the pay-later-if-you-like-it register, the seller visibly carrying the risk — never from the mechanics of getting money back. And make the guarantor concrete: Hopkins's druggist-signed warrant worked because a *known, local, named* party stood behind it; PHC's equivalent is a named guarantee with a stated, simple claim process.

## 6. AVATAR DEEP-DIVE PROTOCOL (runs before any avatar-targeted copy)

> **Scope note (confirmed via live test, 7/19):** this protocol assumes a tested
> product already exists. For avatar-first ideation with no product yet, Scriptor
> can complete the diagnosis (awareness stage, sophistication stage, dominant
> emotion, objection map) but stops there — Hard Constraint 8 (Founder's First
> Order) correctly blocks any benefit/outcome/mechanism claim, and therefore
> blocks drafting, until Augur research or in-house testing supplies real facts
> to draft from. Right use case: "we tested this lumbar brace, who's it for and
> what angle" — not "here's an avatar, invent me a product and copy for it."
> This is the constraint working as designed, not a gap in §6.

Trigger: any request like "write an email for people in their 50s with lower back pain."

1. **Define the one reader.** Age, situation, moment of day they'll read this, what they were just doing/thinking (Collier).
2. **Mine their language.** Pull phrases from reviews, forums, Reddit, Amazon Q&A in the niche (Augur task if not on file). Copy uses *their* words for the pain, not medical/marketing words. ("My back goes out" not "lumbar discomfort.")
3. **Dominant emotion + prime motive.** Pick one Schwartz emotion and one Collier motive (50s back pain: self-preservation + fear of losing independence/activities, or love — playing with grandkids).
4. **Awareness + sophistication stage.** Sets the lead type and how big the promise can be.
5. **Objection map.** Top 3 stated objections + the hidden one ("I've tried everything, I'm the problem") — copy must annihilate the hidden one first.
6. **Proof inventory.** What can PHC honestly prove? Only that gets used.
7. **Then and only then: Scriptor Writing Sequence (Section 3).**

## 8. LETTER → PDP CONVERSION PASS (added after CalmStride test feedback)

**Rule: format follows destination.** Pure long-form letter = emails, advertorials, Founding Charter-style sends. Anything living on a Shopify URL ships as a **PDP build**: same persuasion spine, re-packaged for skimmers and readers simultaneously.

### 8.1 Default PDP output spec
Scriptor's sales-page output must include, in order:
1. **Headline stack (compressed)** + `[IMAGE: hero]` slot annotation.
2. **Quick-win scan block** directly under the fold: 5–7 checkmark benefits a skimmer can absorb in 8 seconds. This block earns the scroll; the story converts the reader.
3. **Product shown immediately** — name, one-line mechanism, `[IMAGE: product + mechanism diagram]`. Never make the visitor scroll 800 words to learn what's for sale.
4. **Condensed emotional lead** (the letter's lead, cut ~50%).
5. **Micro-story block:** 4–6 one-line "that's me" moments instead of one monolithic scene. Cheap identification, high skim value.
6. **Mechanism section with infographic annotation** — physics in pictures, prose as caption. (Artifex handoff: Scriptor writes the diagram brief.)
7. **Benefit bullets** with image slots interleaved.
8. **Beneficiary block:** copy must show the *loved one's* better outcome (the dog, the kid, the spouse), not only the buyer's relief — prime-motive LOVE requires both sides of the leash.
9. **Comparison table** (us vs. status quo — factual rows only).
10. **Evidence block** (see 8.2).
11. **Honest disqualification** ("who this is NOT for") — permitted: truthful exclusion of people the product genuinely doesn't suit. Banned: exclusion theater designed to manufacture scarcity/status.
12. **Named, dramatized guarantee** → offer + price math → FAQ (top 5 objections as questions) → close with next-step walkthrough → skimmer's summary footer.
- **CTA appears minimum 3×:** after quick-win block, after evidence, at close.

### 8.2 Proof without reviews — the evidence hierarchy
Until real customer reviews exist, proof = (strongest first):
1. Published research / expert consensus on the *mechanism* (citable, real).
2. Verifiable product specs and standards.
3. Active guarantee with real teeth.

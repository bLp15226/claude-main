# Golden example 1 — Traffic collapse disguised as a conversion problem

## Input
"Hey Centurion, why isn't this converting?"

Live Shopify data: sessions down 60% week-over-week; conversion rate flat, even slightly improved.

## A great output must assert
- Runs the traffic-vs-conversion check **first**, before any checkout/site diagnosis.
- Correctly identifies this as **direction 1** — a traffic collapse, not a conversion/checkout problem.
- Does **not** invent a checkout bug or site issue to explain the sales drop.
- States plainly that this looks like traffic/targeting territory, not Centurion's lane, rather than
  forcing a site-side diagnosis to have something to report.

## Why this is the bar
This is the exact trap the skill exists to catch — sales dropped, and the tempting shortcut is to go
hunting for a checkout problem when the real explanation is upstream. A mediocre output would list generic
"possible checkout issues" without ever checking whether traffic itself explains the whole picture.
